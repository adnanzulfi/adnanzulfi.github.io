About Me

I turn engineering requirements into precise, manufacturable CAD — parts built to pass inspection the first time.

My edge comes from an unusual background: before I designed parts, I spent years inspecting them. Working inside AS9100 and ISO 9001 standards, running CMM inspections, and checking GD&T on components with zero margin for error, I learned exactly where designs fail — the tolerance that won't hold, the feature that can't be machined, the drawing that doesn't survive the floor.

Now I design with that knowledge built in. Across SolidWorks and AutoCAD, I model sheet metal, weldments, assemblies, and full drawing packages — work that began with rehabilitation exoskeletons and a Class I surgical device in university, and has grown into a portfolio I build on every week.

Grounded in a Biomedical Engineering degree and real manufacturing experience, I bring something most designers can't: I understand both sides of the drawing. My goal is to grow into a CAD and mechanical design engineer who drives innovation — one precise design at a time.
